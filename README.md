# Rules and Gameplay of Durak

## Setup

### Equipment

The game is played using a standard deck of cards from which the numerical 2 through 5 cards have been removed, resulting in 48 total cards.

### Starting Configuration

Each player is dealt 6 cards, with the undealt cards being placed in a pile, and the bottom-most card turned face up, denoting the trump suit. This bottom card is still a part of the undealt pile, and will be the last card drawn in that round.

_If any player has all red, black or 5 cards of the same suit, cards must be dealt again._

## Game Play

### Player Order

The player with the lowest trump card will be the first attacker, and player to the left of the attacker is the defender. Only one person defends at a time, but all other players can join in on the attack. Each round of defending and attacking proceeds clockwise, and if the attacker succeeds and the defender takes the cards, the defender loses their attacking turn and play proceeds to the player on the defender's left. The gameplay will continue with the series of attacking and defending until all but one player is left with cards in their hand.

### Card Rank and Rules

Cards are ranked in ascending order, with Ace being the highest card. A trump-suited card beats all cards in the other three suits. No player can examine the discard pile at any point during the game.

### Attacking

An attack starts with the attacker placing a card in front of the defender. The attacker may place additional cards for the defender, as long as they are the same rank. For example, if the attacker has multiple 6's he or she can place them all at once or consecutively after the defender places a card down against the first 6. At any point during the attack/defence phase, other players can join in on the attack, as long as they have the same ranked card in their deck. For example, if the attacker places a 6 of hearts as the initial card, and the defender lays down an 8 of hearts to beat it, any player can now add either a 6 or an 8, no matter the suit. The maximum number of cards that the defender can defend against is the number of cards in their hand - 6 to begin with, but once the undealt cards are gone, the amount of cards in the defender's hand will decrease. At the end of the attack/defence phase, the attacker draws cards first, then the other attackers if they contributed, and then the defender last. Each time a player draws cards from the undealt pile, they draw until they are back to six cards in hand, or the undealt pile is empty.

### Defending

Once an attacking card is played, the defender attempts to beat it by playing a higher-ranked card of the same suit, or a trump-suited card. For example, if the attacker places a 7 of hearts, the defender can beat it by playing any 8 through Ace of hearts, or any trump-suited card. The defending card is placed on top of the corresponding attacking card. If the defender is successful in defending against the attack, the cards played are overturned and placed in the discard pile. Then, the defender begins their attack phase on the player to his or her left. If the defender is unwilling or cannot beat an attack, he or she must take the cards placed in front of them and add them to his or her hand. If the defender takes the cards, he or she forfeits their attacking turn, and the player to the left of him or her starts their attack on the player to their left.

### Winning and Losing

In most variants of the game, there is no definitive winner, just one loser - the last person with cards in their hand. However, for this project, the winner will be the first player to empty their hand and leave the game, and the loser will be the last one left with cards in their hand.

### Game Strategies

Default Player Behavior: The default player behavior will always play the lowest-ranked possible card(s) in his hand at all times. The default player will not add cards to other players' attacks. During the attacking phase, he will play the lowest-ranked card, or cards if he has multiple of the same rank. Similarly, on defense, he will defend with the lowest possible card in his hand, and if he can't defend with any in his hand, he will take the attacking card and forfeit his attacking turn.

Trump Hoarder: The Trump Hoarder player strategy focuses on retaining trump cards for a late-game advantage. This player will play the lowest-ranked possible card, similar to the default strategy, but when faced with using a trump card to defend himself, he will take the cards and forfeit his turn. Additionally, if he is attacked with a trump card, he will automatically take it and forfeit his turn. However, once the draw cards pile is empty, he will begin to play his trump cards for attacking and defending.

Aggressive Attacker: This player strategy will always play the highest possible card when attacking and will resort to the default strategy when defending. He will also add cards to others' attacks whenever possible.

Chaotic Player: When this player attacks, he will choose a card from his hand randomly to use for the attack. When he defends, he will choose a random card from the cards that he can use to defend himself. For example, if he is attacked with a 6Heart, and he has 7Heart, 9Heart, and QHeart in his hand, he will choose randomly between those three available cards to defend himself.
